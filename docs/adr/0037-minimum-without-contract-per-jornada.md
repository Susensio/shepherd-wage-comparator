# ADR-0037: Measure a job without a contract of up to four weeks per jornada worked, and a longer one as fijo, instead of always as fijo

Status: Accepted (owner, 18 November 2026; boundary corrected the same day after legal research)
Date: 2026-11-18
Amends: ADR-0035 (its round-15 rule «no contract is measured as fijo, ET 15.4»)
Evidence: kb/reports/minimum-wage/es-2026.md (primary texts read on the BOE; inferences marked)

## Context

Since round 15 every job without a contract was measured as fijo: the monthly minimum, vacation as a permanent job, days worked instead of resting as «jornadas de más». An audit on 17 November 2026 found that ET 15.4 makes a worker without registration fijo only after a time equal to the trial period, so the rule overreached on the shortest jobs. The owner found a vacation row on a seven-day cash job senseless, asked how a temporero's minimum compares with a fijo's, and asked to follow the law.

What the law says (report, with quotes):
- Per jornada, RD SMI art. 4.1 ¶1: workers whose services to one company do not exceed 120 days are paid, with the daily SMI, the share of Sundays, holidays and the two extra pays, and never less than `casual` per jornada legal (56,08 € in 2025, 57,82 € in 2026). The 2025 decree names «eventuales, temporeras y temporeros»; the 2026 decree names «contratos de trabajo de duración determinada». Part time is paid pro rata (art. 1). The decree gives no divisor and no hours.
- Vacation of those workers, art. 4.1 ¶2: paid on top («conjuntamente»), as the share of the 30 legal days of the daily SMI for the time under contract (about 3,35 € per calendar day in 2026, an inference).
- A fixed-term job of more than four weeks without a written contract is presumed indefinite and full time unless the employer proves it temporary (ET 8.2). A temporary worker not registered becomes fijo after a time equal to the trial period (ET 15.4 ¶2): at most one month for a fixed-term contract of six months or less (ET 14.1 ¶2), one month for a pastor in the Huesca agreement (art. 39). No Supreme Court ruling after the 2021 reform was found on either point.
- Weekly rest is a day and a half, accumulable up to 14 days (ET 37.1); in livestock work the half day can be accumulated over four weeks (RD 1561/1995 art. 5.3). Hours over the ordinary maximum are overtime (ET 35.1), at least the ordinary hour or rest; the Huesca agreement pays them +75 % (+100 % on holidays, art. 35).
- Inference, not decree text: the per-jornada figure behaves as the daily SMI × 395 paid days / 278 jornadas (a six-day week, holidays and vacation off). A fijo who rests a day and a half earns more per day worked; with the temporero's vacation counted, the gap is about 2–9 %.

Weighed and discarded:
- Always fijo (the round-15 rule): states more than ET 15.4 says for the shortest jobs.
- Per jornada up to 120 days (this ADR's first version): 120 days decides which minimum a valid temporary contract has; it is not when a worker without registration or written contract becomes fijo.
- Always per jornada: understates a longer job without registration, which the law makes fijo.

## Decision

- A job without a contract of up to four weeks (28 calendar days) is measured per jornada worked: «cada día trabajado, al menos <casual> € (Real Decreto del salario mínimo, art. 4.1)». Longer, it is measured as fijo: the monthly minimum, with days worked instead of resting as «jornadas de más» (Estatuto, arts. 8.2 and 15.4).
- A temporary contract keeps the existing rule: per jornada up to `casualMaxDays` (120) of services to the same company, monthly beyond.
- A rest day worked is at least one more jornada (the state floor); where an agreement prices overtime higher (Huesca), the agreement applies — today only noted, its premium is in the backlog — and the rest itself is still owed.
- Without a contract, vacation is not asked and does not enter Corresponde, Cobrado or Falta (owner, 18 November 2026). The decree says it is paid («percibirán»); the page names it once, in «¿Se cumplió la ley?» and Vía legal, with its days and amount, as a right to claim. This is a product choice, stated as such.
- Inferences from the report (the divisor, the 2–9 % gap) are labelled as the page's reading wherever they are shown; citations say «art. 4.1».

## Consequences

- The legal claim is never broader than the text it cites; a short cash job reads as days worked times a figure, with no vacation row.
- The four-week boundary can move a job's minimum by a few euros; the ficha names the rule that applies.
- The Leyes page explains the two minimums with the report's figures, marked as the page's reading where the decree is silent.
- The Huesca overtime premium remains to be modelled (BACKLOG).
