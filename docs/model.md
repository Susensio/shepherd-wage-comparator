# What the model means

The code in `src/model/` is the exact version, and `src/law.js` holds every legal figure with its source. This page keeps only what the code cannot say by itself: what the figures mean and the rules that cross files.

## The offer

- An offer is `{ id, terms, mine }` (ADR-0007), plus `cmp`, the Comparar toggle, which is a view choice and stays at the top.
- `terms` is what the employer offers and the only part ever shared: dates, duration, hours, days off, contract, pay, extra pays, vacation, cash, agreed total, overnight, housing, meals given, the km rate paid, where the job is (the village `place`, INE municipality code and province code), whether it was only an offer and never worked (`offerOnly`) (both since schema version 3, ADR-0028) and the herd (head counts of sheep, goats and cattle). `DEF_TERMS` in `src/offer.js` lists them.
- `mine` is the worker's own figures for that offer: `name`, distance and trips, car, commute, other expenses, the rent override and the IRPF choice. `DEF_MINE` lists them. A field belongs to exactly one of the two.
- Whatever the employer's terms alone decide (schedule, legal minimum, the questions in `offer.js`) takes `terms`; only the car, the other expenses, the rent and the IRPF choice read `mine`.
- `vacationAuto` (vacation set for a short contract, not by the user) sits in `terms` with `vacation`.

## Terms-only figures

- `calcTermsOnly` (`model/calc.js`) calculates an offer from `terms` alone, with `termsOnly` (`offer.js`): a neutral `mine` (no car, so no trips, km or fuel; no other expenses; no rent override; automatic IRPF) and IRPF as if nothing else was earned that year (`others` 0, no other offers). It works from `{ id, terms }`, which is how other people's offers are calculated (ADR-0007).
- The rest of the settings are kept: what housing and meals are worth, Social Security rates. They value the terms; they say nothing about the worker's life. The km rate the employer pays is in `terms` but is paid per km driven at work, so with no km it is zero.
- These figures say what the job pays, not what it is worth to any one worker, so they leave out costs that differ between workers. The Comparar tab can show them for own offers (the «Solo las condiciones» switch).

## Other shepherds' offers

- Read only when signed in with sharing on, fetched on entering the Otros pastores tab and on demand, and kept in `state.others`, in memory (ADR-0030). They are never put in `state.offers`, `localStorage`, backups or the account's own rows.
- They come through `ingestShared` (`schema.js`): defaults for missing fields, unknown ones kept at the current version and dropped from a newer one, no `mine` but the sharer's name. Calculated with `calcTermsOnly` only; the Otros pastores tab ranks them together with your own offers, on terms alone for every row (ADR-0013); Comparar has only your own.
- The read-only view reuses `resultView` with `readOnly`: no editing, negotiating, copying or payslip controls.

## The account copy

- Signed in, the account is the source of truth and `localStorage` its cache (`storage/account.js`). Changes are written after a pause, and only the offers whose `terms`, `mine` or `cmp` changed.
- The first sign-in of a user on a browser uploads the offers the account lacks and keeps both sets, merged by id; ids that are not uuids get a new one first, in the browser too. Only a failed upload asks (ADR-0009). An empty account starts like a first visit, with the templates, and its placeholder offer is never written.
- Backups made before ADR-0007 hold flat offers; the version 1 upgrader (see «Saved format») splits them by `DEF_MINE` (own figures) and puts the rest in `terms`. The browser's own storage of that time (`cj.ofertas`) is still not read.
- A browser that has synced with that user before takes the account's copy as it is: an offer missing from the account was deleted on another device, except what was changed here and not yet written: offline edits, and edits made between opening the page and the account connecting. From the start of a visit with a saved session (`trackUntilConnected`, before the bundle loads), changes go into the same unsynced record (`UNSYNCED_KEY`) as offline ones, and the account's copy keeps them: edited and new offers stay, deleted ones stay deleted, settings stay.
- While a saved session connects, the page shows the cached copy, the account button «connecting» and «Cargando tu cuenta…», never a guest's sign-in; if connecting fails, it falls back to guest with «No se pudo conectar con la cuenta.».
- The blank placeholder offer of a first visit is never written.

## What is owed

- Counted per item (salary, overnight bonus, vacation) and month by month. A surplus in one item does not cover a shortfall in another (ADR-0004).
- **The overnight bonus is an agreement's, known per province** (`overnightOf(province)` in `src/law.js`, reading the verified row `overnight_pct` of `kb/tables/agreement_terms.csv` through `agreementOf`; province code of `terms.province`; today only `HU`, 25 % of the salary, Convenio colectivo del sector agrícola de la provincia de Huesca 2022–2025, art. 28 (BOPH nº 131, 9 July 2024, in force provisionally past 2025 by its art. 5; linked)). Spain has no general rule for it, so the page counts it (minimum, `settle`, ledgers, `calc.debt`, the Para negociar levers, the claim and the card texts) only where the lookup returns an entry for the offer's province, and claims nothing more than the entitlement. With another province, or none, the night stay stays a fact of the offer (`r.min.overnightStay`), `r.min.overnight` and `overnightRate` are 0, and the screens say nothing about it (no row in «¿Es legal?», no hint in the entry: «si no toca, no toca»), so none is counted. The base is the minimum salary: where an agreement's salary is below the minimum wage the agreement is out of date and the minimum wage governs (owner's rule, 15 November 2026), as the model already does; the agreement's own tables are not modelled. A saved offer with a night stay and no province therefore loses the bonus until a place is picked.
- Cash is subtracted from what is owed, but a payroll below the minimum is still illegal, because cash pays no Social Security.
- Without a contract there is no payroll: everything paid is cash, measured against a fixed contract's minimum (ET 15.4). The vacation question is asked too, and a fixed job's vacation cannot be replaced by money (ET 38.1): `taken` (days off, `min.vacation` 0) and `paidAtEnd` (`r.vacationApart`: the minimum vacation, paid apart in cash on top of the cash agreed, counted in `earnings.parts.vacationPay` and, in `settle`, as agreed vacation) owe nothing on the ficha; `included` or no answer still owe `min.vacation`, and the ficha says why (`NO_CONTRACT_VACATION`). Paid apart is a fact of the terms: with nothing cobrado in the record, `Corresponde` includes it and `Falta` carries it. The day marks of the record do not change this: the vacation follows the terms, as with a contract. Under «Lo pactado» the vacation paid apart is not cobrado (nobody agreed it in money): it stays in Falta, and `lawFromRecord` does not judge the minimum on payroll (it reads no payslip; what the minimum adds is in Falta). The summary for the ganadero says «Cobrado: lo pactado, N netos», never «nada».

## What happened: phases and settling (ADR-0032)

- `model/phase.js` owns what a job is: `isWorked` (dated and not `offerOnly`; `workedOffers` and `overlapsOf` read it), `jobStart`, `jobEnd`, `phaseOf` and `hasFacts` (a day, a cobro or an answer to «¿Se cobró?» recorded). `model/days.js` owns `daysBetween`, `monthDays`, `monthSpan`, `monthFacts` (the one reader of a month's days, per day or counted) and `spreadTotal`. `phaseOf(offer, today)`: `offerOnly` if `terms.offerOnly`; `undecided` without a start date or with one after today; `finished` when the end (`endOf`) is known and before today; otherwise `underway`, so an offer with no known duration stays underway. Starting or ending today is still underway.
- `settle(offer, record, today, r)` (`model/settle.js`) is pure and takes the offer's figures `r` (what `calc` gave) instead of computing them; `monthAccounts(offer, record, key, today, settled, r)` takes the settled months the same way. `calcOffer(o)` and `settleOffer(o)` in `state.js` memoise both per offer until a save or the next paint (`resetFigures`), so the views read the same figures instead of recomputing them. The record is `{ months: { 'YYYY-MM': { closed, days: { 'DD': 'work' | 'off' | 'sick' } } }, payments: [{ id, date, amount, via, note? }] }`; missing parts mean empty. A libranza and a vacation day are the same mark, «off»: vacation is not a day state. `amount` is net: what actually arrived (payroll after withholdings, cash as handed). Without a start date there are no months.
- A **closed month** takes its days as listed (an unlisted day is not worked); the offer cannot change it. In an **open month** a listed day is a fact and the rest follows the offer (`facts.followingOffer` is that share of the month), and the row is flagged `estimate`. Month shares are the payslips' (`monthShares`: partial months at 1/30), so the months add up to the contract. With a start but no known end, months run from the start to the month of `today` (the month in progress is an open row, due at its end); each is one month's pay (`calc` counts one month without a duration), the first at 1/30 a day if it starts mid-month, and the vacation accrues month by month instead of falling due at the end.
- What the days change: a wage «al día» is the day rate times the paid days of the month (worked, plus the vacation taken and the holidays, which follow the offer even in a closed month); the minimum a casual contract is judged by follows the days worked. A monthly or total wage does not shrink with rest days. Days worked above the month's due workdays are `extraDays`, valued at the minimum wage and kept apart: they are not in `owed`.
- Per month and per item (salary, overnight bonus, vacation, the vacation falling due in the last month): `agreed`, `minimum`, `due` = the greater of the two, `shortfall` = what the minimum exceeds the agreed pay (the ficha's debt), `received` and `owed` = due minus received. Nothing is netted across items or months (ADR-0004). The agreed pay covers the minimum salary first, then the overnight bonus, and it is payroll plus the agreed cash: each month's cash (`row.agreedCash`, net as handed; per day it follows the paid days of that month (the days worked, facts of a closed month included, plus the vacation taken and the holidays, as the payroll's daily wage does; a net total per day, the days worked alone), else it is the month's weight like the pay) enters the pool as the gross that brings it net, the figure `calc` credits (its bisection, `r.cash.grossEq`, shared out over the cash; `/ r.netFactor` without a contract, as `calc` does for that debt), so total remuneration is what is compared with the minimum, as `calc` does with `coveredByCash`. Without a contract the pay is only the cash. Cash received still reduces `owed` (a cash payment is grossed up by the same ratio, so paying exactly what was agreed leaves nothing of it owed; payroll payments by `r.netFactor`), and `belowMinimumOnPayroll` stays on the declared pay alone. Cash above what a month's minimum salary and bonus need covers a vacation shortfall, as in `calc`: that much moves from the salary item to the vacation, so the total due is the pay agreed (not with a contract absent: the whole vacation is owed whatever the cash). The vacation owed comes from the offer's terms, as in `calc`, never from the days.
- **Severance** (`severance`, the fourth item, ET 49.1.c): a temporary contract with a known duration owes `calc`'s severance figure (`r.employer.severance`, 12 days of salary a year) at its real end, in the last row, in proportion to the job as it really was (the sum of the rows' weights: a job that ends early earns less); the offer does not promise it, so `agreed` is 0 and `minimum` = `due` = the figure. A quit (`record.end.how === 'quit'`) earns none. Under way it is not counted (`standingOf`: it falls due with the end). It is not part of the pay's minimum (`minimumNote` (its `unit` is the one the pay was agreed in: `día`, `mes` or `total`) and the minimum on payroll of `lawFromRecord` leave it out) nor of the ficha's debt (`r.debt`). Net it is the same as gross: the severance is neither taxed nor contributed on up to its legal amount (LGSS 147.2.c, LIRPF 7.e), so `netOfItem`/`netOfRow` (`settle.js`) leave it out of `netFactor` everywhere it is shown, and a cobro covers it at its net amount. It is in Corresponde, Falta, the message, the letter, the papeleta and the Expediente like any item, and «Todo cobrado» waits for it.
- **Vacation taken, job ended early** (ET 38.1): an offer with vacation «taken» owes nothing for it while the job runs to its end, but if `jobEnd` is before the offer's end the days accrued (`LAW.vacationDaysPerYear` over the real months) and not taken are owed at the end, at one day of the minimum wage (`min.vacationDay`). The days taken are the offer's last ones, so those after the real end were not taken. The default calendar lays them out the same way: the last `vacationTaken` days of the offer are libre (`marksOf`), and the other days off are spread over the rest, so a record identical to the offer has no days more or less.
- Payments, cash included, are pooled, oldest first by date, and fill the months in order (salary, overnight, vacation within each; a payment names no item, so the split by item is by convention and the total owed does not depend on it). Payments are net, so each is grossed up as `calc` credits cash (`amount / r.netFactor`) before it is compared with the gross dues; an excess over everything due is `unassigned`. Cash arrived, so it reduces `owed` (as `coveredByCash` does in `calc`), and `totals.cash` (the «en negro» money) reports it apart, net as handed. Legality is separate: `declared` is the gross equivalent of what was received on payroll (`via: 'payroll'`, the only way that is declared: a payslip paid by bank transfer; `via: 'cash'` is «En mano», en negro), and `belowMinimumOnPayroll` what the minimum exceeds it by, per item and month, because cash pays no Social Security. It is never folded into `owed`. A month with something owed carries `deadline`, `LAW.claimYears` after its due date (the offer's last day for the last month), and `lapsed` once that is before today; `totals.claimable` leaves lapsed months out.
- **The screens** (`model/record.js`, `model/accounts.js`, `model/compliance.js`, `model/job-view.js`, `ui/record.js`, `views/record.js`, `views/calendar.js`; `job-view.js` is the pure part of what the record's views show: `recordPhase`, the pill, the questions at the turns of a job, the months strip, the card, and `ui/record.js` adds what the page remembers and the buttons). Every worked job starts with its agreed «Condiciones». **Registro** (under way): the months strip (cerrado, por cerrar, en curso, por venir), a line pointing to a month that waits to be closed, «Hasta hoy» first, right after «Condiciones» (the tiles Corresponde, Cobrado with «de ello, en mano», Falta, with the notes of what the figures leave to say), then the questions at the turns, «Cobros», the row «Días», the month tapped open below (calendar, counts, what the month corresponds to, «Cerrar <mes>»), and the rest of the offer's analysis in one closed fold (while the start is not confirmed, «¿Empezó el trabajo?» leads, as nothing is known yet). **Cuentas** (over) puts the result first and the chores last, under the small title «Cuentas»: the yellow block (red once «Debe») with the tiles Corresponde, Cobrado and Falta and their notes (while months are not closed, the line that the figure is not firm; the quiet link «Revisar los meses» while months are open, and «¿Se espera algún pago más?» under it, then «Cobros» and the row «Días»; a job finished with nothing recorded and no answer has no result yet, so «¿Cómo acabó?» and «¿Se cobró este trabajo?» come first there); «Balance del trabajo» (`balanceOf`: Corresponde in total, Cobrado split into nómina and en mano, the jornadas, and the days contributed and the paro from the offer; «estimado» or «según la oferta» wherever a figure still follows the offer, and the fold «Detalle de lo que corresponde» with its lines); «¿Se cumplió la ley?» (facts only, see below); «Pedir lo que falta» (the message for the ganadero, one line with the deadline and the button «Vía legal»; see «The claim ladder» below) while Falta is above 0, with no cobro, or after a dismissal; «Registro del trabajo» (the months, each a row that opens its calendar in place, one at a time, with no separate view and no history entry, a closed one to reopen it; «+ Añadir <mes>» the same; «Cobros»), a fold that is open only while a month is not closed or there is no cobro (`data-autofold`: the page sets it, `restoreFolds` leaves it); and the closed fold «Oferta original» (Desglose, ¿Es legal?, Final de campaña with its simulator, Cómo se calcula; not «Para negociar»), a reference and not part of the result. «Por decidir», «solo oferta» and an offer with something missing keep the headline and the full analysis. A day on the calendar is what the record holds or else the offer's default (`defaultKinds`: the days off spread evenly over the month's span); a tap flips jornada and libre, and the first tap in a month records every day as shown (`toggleDay`), dropping the month again if every day is back to the default. Closing records every day as shown and freezes the month (`closeMonth`), only once it has ended and from its calendar or «Revisar los meses»; `reopenMonth` keeps the days and asks to confirm. «Baja» has no control: a `sick` day already in the record is shown and a tap makes it a jornada. There is no list of holidays, so festivos are not marked. Where the days marked make the work run otherwise than the offer's dates, one line near «Condiciones» says «Trabajo real: del <fecha> al <fecha> (oferta: <fecha>)» (derived, nothing to edit); the strip and the registro's months include the months after the offer's end that hold a day worked, and after the last month shown, once the next month has started, a quiet «+ Añadir <mes>» (`addableMonth`) opens that month's calendar with every day libre: it is not part of the job until a day is marked worked in it, and has nothing to close. The claim text says the days worked after the offer's end. The calendar compares with the offer by counts of jornadas (`monthAccounts`): the difference is shown only when they differ, valued as the month settled with the offer's own days written out against the month as it is. «Revisar los meses» has a history entry (`openReview`/`closeReview`), the strip and the calendar none.
- **Today** comes from `todayISO()` (`offer.js`, local date), the only place the page asks the date; the e2e tests fix it with `page.clock`.
- **The job as it really was** (`jobShares` in `model/settle.js`, `lastWorkedDay` in `model/record.js`, `jobEnd` and `phaseOf(offer, today, record)` in `model/phase.js`). The record has no dates of its own and nothing is saved for this: the real period is derived from the days marked in `mine.record.months`. A month after the offer's last one enters the job only if it holds a day marked `work` (an entry with none is ignored, as if absent); the job then goes on to the last day worked, the months between included, whose days default to libre. Each day of that period (from the day after the offer's last day to the last day worked) is paid 1/30 of a month's pay (`LAW.payrollMonthDays`, as the payslips prorate a partial month: a whole month is 1): a wage per day is the day rate times the days worked, a monthly or total wage is one thirtieth per calendar day, rest days inside the period paid. The cash, the overnight bonus, the minimum, the vacation accrued (`vacationShareOf`: every month's weight, due at the end) and the paid vacation taken and holidays of a wage per day follow the same weight, over the offer's own shares; a net total agreed scales by the days as before. Nothing is trimmed inside the offer's dates: libre days at the start or end of a closed month (two rests on 27–28 February) are part of the job and the monthly pay is whole; the job ends early only by the end the shepherd said (`record.end`). `settle().job` is the real period (`from`, `to`, the offer's, `differs`). `row.calendarFrom`/`calendarTo` are the days the calendar shows (the whole month for the last one, so a day after the offer's end can be marked), `row.extension` flags a month after the offer's. The end is one function, `jobEnd(offer, record)` in `model/phase.js`, used by the phase and by `jobShares`: the end the shepherd said (else the offer's), or the last day worked when that is later. An end said after the offer's extends the job, and the days up to it are worked unless marked otherwise (the calendar shows them `work`); the phase is `finished` only if it is before today; the start stays the offer's.
- **The month in progress counts up to today** (`settle`, `standingOf`). The share of the month that contains `today` is capped at `today` (`shareOf`): a monthly or total wage is one thirtieth per elapsed day (the payslips' partial month), a wage per day is the day rate times the days worked so far (the facts marked plus the offer's pattern for the unmarked elapsed days), the cash agreed and the minimum follow the same share, and the vacation accrues in proportion (`standingOf` takes its share of the job counted so far). «Hasta hoy» counts the months that have ended plus that one; `row.share` and `row.fullShare` say how much of it is counted. Closed months and a job that is over are unchanged. The pattern of the days still following the offer is the offer's days worked over its calendar days (`workPattern`), so a month of 28 days is worth 28 days as the calendar shows; the average month of an offer with no end keeps its share.
- **The big cards of «¿Es legal?»** (jornadas de más, horas extra, what the ganadero owes, and without a contract the paro that is not generated) are all folds (`details.owed`, `data-fold=owed`): the figure and the stamp in the summary, the reasons and what makes it legal inside, closed by default, the open state kept by the named fold.
- **Days outside the job** (`Mark.outside`, `row.jobFrom`/`jobTo`): the days of a calendar month after the job's end (the offer's, or the last day worked) or before its start, are not libranzas. The calendar fades them, labels them «fuera» and leaves them out of the counts line and the money; a tap marks one jornada, which extends the job. Vacation at the end needs no mark: the vacation owed comes from the offer's terms.
- **Net, and per day exactly**: every figure of Registro and Cuentas is net. A net total agreed per day with the vacation inside it is that amount for each day worked, vacation included: the vacation accrues, and its minimum with it, in each month with the days worked there (`settle().vacationByDays`), so a month with 8 jornadas at 70 € net reads 560 € and one more jornada +70 €. Gross is only in the claim text.
- **Why Corresponde is above the pay agreed** (`minimumNote`): the minimum is owed item by item (ADR-0004), so a line appears wherever an item falls short. Where the minimum as a whole (salary minimum, plus the overnight bonus where the province's agreement gives it and there is a night stay, plus the vacation where owed) exceeds the pay agreed (`exceeds`), the line says what it is made of, in net and in one unit (per day, or per month), against the pactado. Where the total does not exceed the pay but an item still falls short (`short`), the line names the item («Las vacaciones no se pagan aparte: corresponden X €/día netos más»), except the vacation of a job with no contract, which says nothing the missing contract has not said: a minimum below the pay agreed is never printed as if it exceeded it. Nowhere else.
- **Overtime is one quantity a week**: `r.extra.overtimePerWeek`, the hours beyond the normal workday on the days worked, called «horas extra» on the card of «¿Es legal?», in the claim, in the card text, in the summary of the hours right and in the lever. The lever's «sin pagar» line is the unpaid part of it (`overtimeUnpaidPerWeek`). The hours above 40 a week, which add the days worked beyond the rest due, exist only inside the weekly-hours chart, as «de más» with that explanation under it.
- **Money of a year** (`yearMoney`): the headers of the year in Ofertas and «Año a año» in Comparar keep two sums apart: «cobrados», the cobros recorded and dated in that year, and «según las ofertas», `earnings.received` of each worked offer split by days. They are never added, and for a job with cobros the second is only a reference.
- **Totals, not months** (`standingOf`, `model/accounts.js`). The job is worked with its totals: Corresponde − Cobrado = Falta, in net, `falta` never below 0 and the same word in Registro and Cuentas. Months matter only for the days that make Corresponde (a month is closed after it has ended, from its calendar); a cobro is a free entry that belongs to no month and covers none. What was cobrado beyond Corresponde (`sobra`) is a note that real payslips differ from the simulated ones, never a debt or «de más». Under way Corresponde is the months that have ended (the current month so far and the vacation are aside, payable later); once over it is the whole job. With no cobro in the record no Falta is shown, only Corresponde and a prompt, and `belowMinimumOnPayroll` is not mentioned.
- **Net** (`standingOf`, `monthAccounts`): the owner thinks in what reaches the shepherd, so what `settle` has gross is taken times `r.netFactor`, the cash agreed is the cash as handed (its gross, `agreedCashGross`, is taken out of the items first, salary first, so the lines add up), and the cobros are as they arrived. A **net total agreed** (`cashMode: 'total'`, the pay answers' «total pactado», split by `effectiveOffer` into a payroll whose net is the simulated one and a cash that is the rest) comes out as that total with no simulated withholding left in it: the payroll gross × `netFactor` is the payroll net by construction and the cash is as handed. Agreed per day, it follows the days worked alone, payroll and cash together (`r.cash.unit` keeps `'day'`, and `settle` pays the payroll per day worked, the vacation taken being inside the total), so each day worked less in a closed month takes that day's net off, down to the legal minimum, which is compared in gross and tops up. Per month or in total it follows the month's weight. Gross is only in the claim text (`recordClaimText`), where it is what is claimed, and the text says so.
- **Deadlines**: `settle` assigns the payments oldest first, but the claim (ET 59.2) is dated from the earliest month the cobros dated on or before its due date did not cover (the first owed row takes that month's deadline), so a late-paid October is not dated by March; the total owed does not change. After a dismissal with money owed the papeleta's step has the earlier of the dismissal's deadline and the amounts'. They feed: `standingOf().nearest` (the first deadline of a month with something open, nothing when nothing is missing) and `deadlines` (per month, for the fold «Plazos por mes» in the page «Vía legal», opened by its button from «Pedir lo que falta» with a history entry of its own: everything legal is there, not in Cuentas). No month is accused in the main view.
- **The law, from the record** (`model/compliance.js`, `lawFromRecord`): facts only. Weekly rest and days worked above the due workdays look at CLOSED months alone: the rest counts days marked off per period of `LAW.restPeriodWeeks` weeks from the job's start, judging only the whole periods wholly inside closed months (`rest.periods` says how many; a last period shorter than the weeks it groups over is not judged), against `LAW.weeklyRest` a week on average and, because only the half day can accumulate (RD 1561/1995 art. 5.3), a full day off in each week or two in each 14 days (a fail with `weekly: true` is short of full days even when the total is enough); the days worked are those of closed months against the model's own due workdays (`r.extra.due`) in the share of the job that is closed. The screen says «sobre N de M meses cerrados», and with no month closed is one line, «Cierra los meses para comprobarlo». The overnight bonus (against `totals.items.overnight.owed`) and the minimum on payroll are judged only once the shepherd has closed the record (`record.complete`, «Cerrar el registro»: every cobro is recorded) with EVERY month closed (`known`); before that there is no row for them, only one line saying that closing the record checks them. Without a contract there is no minimum on payroll (`applicable`): the facts say it. The minimum compares what arrived on payroll, in net, with the net equivalent of the minimum (the gross minimum of every item times `r.netFactor`), and fails only beyond a margin for withholding differences: the net equivalent less one more simulated IRPF's worth of the gross minimum (`r.pay.irpfPct`), nothing else invented. Not checked, and not mentioned: festivos worked (until holidays exist per municipality and year) and the minimum against the real payslip: only what can be judged for the offer has a row.
- **One job, two sides** (ADR-0035, phase B; `model/happened.js`). `terms` is what was agreed, `mine.record` what happened, and each part of what happened defaults to the agreed one. `happenedOf(offer, today, r)` returns null for an offer only or a job not started; otherwise `{ phase, settled, standing, dates, days, money }`. `dates.from/to` come from `settle().job`, each with its source (`recorded`: `record.started`, `record.end` or a day worked past the offer's end; else `agreed`); `days.work` sums the rows' `worked` (the days recorded plus the offer's pattern for the days still following it) with, per month, how it is kept (`days`, `count` or `agreed`) and `days.source` (`agreed`, `recorded`, `mixed`); `money` is net (`due` = Corresponde, `received` = Cobrado, `missing` = Falta) with its `basis`: `cobros` (the payments, `approx` when one is a remembered lump), `asAgreed`, `none`, or `unknown` (no answer and no cobro: `received` is 0 and Falta is all that is due, to be asked and not claimed). The three ways to enter a job all compute: (a) an offer worked and recorded; (b) a past job known by its conditions, answered «Lo pactado» (`paid: 'asAgreed'`): Cobrado is what was agreed and Falta only what the legal minimum adds to it (`missingByMinimumOnly`, so the screen labels it); (c) a past job known by what happened, with thin terms: a total of days spread into month counts and a lump cobro, possibly approximate. Hours are not needed: without `hoursPerDay` the job is measured as full working days (`LAW.workdayHours`, `time.hoursUnknown`), the law's default when part time is not written down (ET 12.4.a), and the ficha says so under the minimum; no overtime is counted.
- **`actualFigures(offer, today, r)`** gives the figures Comparar will rank, `{ real, earnings: { total, perHour, perDay, perMonth }, time }`, in the shape of `calc`'s `earnings` (net). Real (`real: true`) only for a job worked, finished and with `record.complete` (the shepherd's word that the record is whole; every month closed is not asked, since a past job entered by totals has months of counts that nobody closes): `total` is what was received (`money.received`) plus what is not money that reaches the shepherd in the agreed figures (housing, meals, severance, minus expenses), over the days worked, the months the job lasted (the sum of the rows' `fullShare`) and the hours of those days (`days × hoursPerDay`, full days without hours). Any other job gives the agreed `earnings` and `time` of `calc`, `real: false`.
- **`record.paid`** (saved format 8, replaces `nothingPaid`): the answer to «¿Se cobró este trabajo?» (Lo pactado, Otra cantidad, Nada; «Otra cantidad» is not a value: it is the cobros, so it records nothing in `paid`). `asAgreed` («Lo pactado»): `settle` takes every item as received what the offer agreed **in money** for it (`received = agreed`, except a vacation paid apart that nobody agreed in money, `r.vacationApart`, which is not received; `owed` = the shortfall under the minimum plus that, `declared` the payroll share of it, `totals.cash` the agreed cash), the claim dated from each month's own due date, and `standingOf` has `cobrado` = Corresponde minus the net shortfall. `none`: nothing was received. Either way the payments are not read, and `addPayment` takes the answer back (a cobro is more specific); absent means unknown and the cobros speak. `setPaid(record, paid)` sets or clears it. `standingOf` also gives `agreed` (Corresponde without what only the law adds) and `legalItems`, the shortfall per item (salary, overnight, vacation, severance) in whole euros that add up to the net total; under «Lo pactado» the screen lists them and «También se cobró» turns the answer into cobros (one payroll cobro of the net Corresponde on the job's last day, plus the cash agreed as an En mano cobro), which is the saved format as it is. A cobro may carry `approx: true`, a lump remembered; it counts as any other and `happenedOf` says it.
- **A month of counts** (saved format 8): `record.months[key] = { closed, closedAt?, count: { work, off, sick } }` keeps the month's days as three whole numbers instead of one mark each; a month holds one encoding, the marks winning if both are saved. `monthFacts(entry, from, to)` in `model/days.js` is the one reader: a counted month accounts for all the days of the job's part of it (`work` cannot pass them, `sick` fills what is left, the rest is `off`) and is not followed by the offer. `marksOf` lays the counts over the days of the job (work, then sick, then off) and flags every mark `counted`, which says nothing of the real days; `toggleDay` has no day to tap and `startSick` leaves a counted month as it is. `setMonthCounts(record, key, counts)` sets one month; `enterTotalDays(record, months, total)` spreads a total of days worked for the whole job (`spreadTotal`, in proportion to the days each month has, whole days, largest remainders first, no month above its days) over the months not closed, the rest of each being days off. **Weekly rest cannot be judged on counted months**: `lawFromRecord` skips every period that holds one (`rest.counted` says how many months are counted, for the screen to say so) and leaves the holidays worked out of them; the days worked still count.
- **What is expected of the money** (`record.complete = { date }`, saved format 7, and `record.expecting = { by? }`, saved format 9; `completeRecord`, `expectPayment` and `clearExpectation` in `model/record.js`; the older «Cerrar el registro» and «Reabrir el registro» are gone, ADR-0032's «closed record» now means `complete`). The state of a finished job's money comes from a question about the future, «¿Se espera algún pago más?», asked under the Cobros of a job finished while Falta is above 0 and neither answer is set: «Sí, quedó en pagarlo» (`expecting`, with the optional day asked in «¿Para cuándo?»; «Sin fecha» leaves it out) or «No» (`complete`: no more cobro is expected). The two exclude each other (setting one clears the other); once answered, one line with «Cambiar» (which clears both) says it. `moneyState` (`model/accounts.js`, from Falta, the record and today) gives the state: Falta 0 is `settled` whatever was said; with Falta, `complete` is `owed`, `expecting` is `expecting` while there is no day or today is not after it and `owed` the day after (the line then reads «Quedó en pagar el <fecha> y no ha llegado.»), and neither answer is `unconfirmed`, which is never a debt to claim. The card and the headline of Falta follow it (`jobStatus`): grey «Por confirmar», amber «Esperando cobro», red «Debe», green «Saldado»; «Prescrito» (grey) wins over all three when every month owed lapsed. Under `expecting` «Hablar con el ganadero» (the summary) leads, open, and the formal claim waits; under `owed` the claim leads. A cobro added later only recalculates: nothing locks or reopens, and if Falta reaches 0 the job is `settled`. `setPaid` with `asAgreed` sets `complete` (taking the answer back with no cobro recorded clears both), `none` leaves the question to answer, and `removePayment` clears both when no cobro or answer is left. A cobro dated after `complete.date` is told «Cobro apuntado el <fecha>, después de dar los cobros por completos.» (only the cobro's date is saved, so a cobro added later with an earlier date is not told). Reopening a month does not clear either: the screens' `complete` is `record.complete` with every month closed (`ctx.complete`). Complete, the figures are firm and the minimum on payroll and the plus de pernocta are judged. Saved `complete` keeps meaning «no se espera más cobro».
- **One reading of «Cobrado»** (`cobradoOf`, `model/job-view.js`): `{ basis, amount, approx }` with `basis` `cobros`, `asAgreed`, `none` or `unknown`. Known, `amount` is `standing.cobrado`; `unknown` is the agreed money (`r.earnings.received`, `approx`) for a finished job and 0 for one still under way, which has not been paid. Cuentas' tile, «Final de campaña» (a card that says «Cobrado» where it is known, the simulated pay otherwise), the year sums of the list (the cobros dated in the year, or the amount split by days and marked «≈») and the Expediente («Se cobró todo lo pactado» under `asAgreed`) read it, so they cannot disagree.
- **Cards**: a phase pill and, for a job worked, a status colour and one short line (`jobStatus`, `cardOf` in `model/job-view.js`; red where the ganadero owes, amber where something remains to record, see «estado de la tarjeta» in the glossary). The cards stay in their year groups: there is no «Para reclamar» group and no next-step line (the deadline is in the ficha).
- Rows are in cents, rounded by running total so each column adds up to its total as shown (`ledger.js`).
- **Invariant** (`test/unit/settle.test.js`): with an empty record, `totals` equal what `calc` shows: agreed pay (`pay.salary` plus the agreed cash as the gross that brings it net, `r.cash.grossEq`; `pay.vacationPay` for the vacation), minimums (`min`), and the total `shortfall` equals the debt after the cash, `r.debt.owed`, to the cent. By item (`debt.parts`) it is compared only where no cash is involved, since `calc` has no item split of what the cash covers. It holds for every sample offer, with or without a contract. Without a contract the pay is only the cash, and the vacation is owed whatever the cash. Self-employed work has no due or debt (ADR-0029).
- Open choices, marked `TODO:` in the code: sick days are not paid; a holiday worked counts twice; extra days are not yet in `owed`.

## The claim ladder (ADR-0034, proposed)

- **Phase**: `jobEnd` takes the end the shepherd said (`record.end.date`) in place of the offer's, then the later of it and the last day worked; so «Acabó antes» finishes a job and a dismissal in an open-ended offer ends it. `settle` ends the job at `jobEnd` when it is before the offer's end (`jobShares`): the months after it leave the job, the last one stops that day, so the final pay (vacation, extra pays) falls due then and its one-year deadline counts from it; the days after are outside the job («fuera», «·» in the Expediente), never libre, whether or not months are closed. A day marked worked after it keeps the job going to that day. An open-ended offer's months stop there too. The texts and the Expediente say the dates of `settled.job`.
- **Saved format 6** (all optional, in `mine`): `record.end = { how: agreed | dismissed | quit, date }` (sick leave is not an end), `record.started = { date }` (the start confirmed; `terms.start` moves when the shepherd gives another date, since it is a fact of the job), `record.months[k].closedAt`, `record.evidence = [{ date, text }]`, `record.nothingPaid` (replaced in format 8 by `record.paid: 'none'`: a flag, never a payment of 0; `standingOf(…, paid)` counts it as cobros said, so Falta is Corresponde; adding a cobro clears it), `mine.parties = { employerName, employerId?, workerName? }`, `mine.claim = { steps: { [id]: { done: ISO, note?, promise? } } }` (`promise`: the date the ganadero promised to pay, on `talk`). `normalizeRecord`, `normalizeParties` and `normalizeClaim` keep what is valid.
- **Steps** (`model/claim.js`, `claimSteps`): `dismissal` (only after a dismissal; with it, `union` and `conciliation` always, owed or not, the papeleta counting to the dismissal's date), `talk`, `request`, `union`, `conciliation`, `inspection`, `court` (ready once the conciliation is marked), `fogasa`. Modes: `settled` (cobros said and Falta 0: no ladder), `noCobros` (only `talk`), `owed`, `dismissed`. `by` is the earliest deadline not lapsed (ET art. 59.2, `LAW.claimYears`); `inWindow` = something owed inside it, or an open dismissal window; `next` = the first step ready and not done, with the date it counts against; with no cobro said, `cobros` (write them down) while a month is not lapsed (`standingOf().firstDeadline`), which also makes the job «Para reclamar»; a promise to pay not yet due is `promise`, waited for.
- **`dismissalDeadline`**: `LAW.dismissalDays` (20) weekdays, Monday to Friday, from the day after the dismissal, holidays ignored. ET art. 59.3 counts working days (and LRJS art. 103.1 leaves out Saturdays, Sundays and the court's holidays), so the date is never later than the real one; the page says «como muy tarde».
- **Texts** (`claim-text.js`): the amounts come from `settle` and `standingOf`. «Resumen para el ganadero» is net in whole euros, as the tiles; the months and items are spread (`spreadTo`, `ledger.js`) to add up to the Falta it says. «Petición de pago» is gross, as the claim, with a pay-by date (default +15 days), and says nothing about stopping a deadline. «Hechos para la Inspección» holds facts and no amount claimed. The fields of the papeleta are prefilled from the record. Under way, «Resumen para el ganadero» lists only the months counted by `standing.counted` (those ended and the one in progress), in the days and in Falta: months not yet due take no part of it.
- **«Copiar reclamación»** in the «⋯» menu: `claimToCopy` (`claim-text.js`) decides from the phase, and the menu's availability reads the same function (`claimCopyOf` in `ui/record.js`): offer only or undecided, the offer's own claim; under way, the talk summary when something is owed, else the entry is hidden; finished, the formal letter when something is owed, else hidden; a dismissal, the papeleta's fields.
- **Expediente** (`model/dossier.js`, `views/dossier.js`): net in whole euros, as the tiles: Corresponde and Falta spread over the months to the tiles' figures, Cobrado what is left of each, so rows and columns add up and the totals are the tiles'; gross is `settle`'s rows in cents. It has a history entry and prints alone (`@media print`).
- **Cuentas saldadas**: cobros said and Falta 0 gives a calm green «Saldado» block (with «Cobrado de más: X €» when more was cobrado than corresponds), no yellow, no red tile, no cash prompt and no «no es firme». The phase pill says «Por empezar» while a job under way has nothing recorded and no answer to «¿Empezó el trabajo?». Under way with months unpaid, «Hablar con el ganadero» (the summary up to today) is a closed fold under «Hasta hoy».
- Not included, not verified: interest for late pay (ET 29.3), limits on pay in kind (ET 26.1), FOGASA ceilings, whether August counts in dismissal deadlines, free legal aid.

## Extra time

Two rules, and each extra hour counts once.

- **Vacation accrues pro rata, exactly**: `time.vacationDays` is `LAW.vacationDaysPerYear × months / 12` with no rounding to whole days (a job of 20 days earns 1,67), in the money and in the early-end accrual of `settle`. The days taken off (`time.vacationTaken`) are whole, because the calendar has no half days.
- **Paid days:** a wage «al día» is paid for the whole days worked (`daysWorked`, rounded) plus the public holidays and vacation taken off, which are paid too (`paidDays` in `model/time.js`). With nothing taken off, pay per day worked is exactly the daily wage.
- **Extra days:** days worked above the due workdays (the contract's days minus weekly rest, public holidays and vacation taken). Each is valued as a normal workday at the minimum wage.
- **Overtime:** hours above the normal workday, on every day worked, valued at the minimum hourly wage. No agreement premium is added (Huesca's art. 35, +75 % / +100 % on holidays, is a verified row but not modelled: it is a claim above the minimum that the model does not make).
- **The hourly minimum** is the year's minimum wage over the annual hours (`min.hourly` in `legalMinimum`, `annualHoursOf(terms.province, year)` in `src/law.js`): the province agreement's `annual_hours` where a verified row exists (Huesca, 1,760, art. 25), else the reference `LAW.annualHours` (1,826, Granada). Everything priced by the hour follows (`extra.dayValue`, `daysValue`, `overtimeValue`, the unpaid amounts and `savings`); the comparison chart's hourly and daily minimum read `min.hourly`. The weekly hours (40) and the 30 natural days of vacation of the Huesca rows equal what the model already uses and are not read from the rows.
- Whatever is paid above the legal minimum, on payroll and in cash, covers the extra days first and the overtime after.

## Earnings and savings

- «Se gana» (`earnings.total`, `perHour`, `perDay`, `perMonth`, and the parts in `PARTS`) is money only: what reaches the worker, plus housing and food when counted, minus car and other expenses. The unemployment benefit a contribution generates is not part of it (ADR-0022): `r.unemployment` keeps its days and euros, shown apart under the headline. The old «Contar el paro que generas» setting is gone; `ingestSaved` drops a saved `unemployment` value.
- **Paro lost to money en negro** (`r.unemployment.lost`): the benefit that the declared pay plus the gross equivalent of the cash (`r.cash.grossEq`) would give, minus the benefit generated, over the same contributed days (a month when there is no duration). Without a contract, all of it. Zero when the declared pay already reaches the benefit's maximum, and without cash. Only the euros differ when there is a contract: the days contributed are the same.
- **Comparar's ranking key** is «Se gana» plus the paro, both in the chosen unit (`inUnit`); with «Solo lo que se gana» (not saved) it is «Se gana» alone. The sum is never shown. Offers without a duration, in «en total», go last. The chart over time, the yearly summary and the year headers of Ofertas use money only.
- The law's figures (Social Security rates, vacation days, annual hours) are read from `src/law.js`, not from the settings; values saved for them are dropped on load (`ingestSaved`).
- **Savings** is what the employer saves at the worker's expense, with their Social Security: the contributions on the cash part, the missing minimum, and the unpaid extra days and overtime. It is the worker's margin to negotiate.

## Agreed totals and net pay

- An agreed total is split into payroll and cash: the cash part is the total minus what reaches the worker on payroll, net, and never below zero.
- A net pay is turned into the gross that gives it, because IRPF depends on the gross. The same conversion says what gross would bring the cash part net.
- IRPF counts the worker's other worked offers in the same year. Those are calculated on their own to avoid circular references.

## Pay questions

The «Pago» section of the form is a view over `terms`; it saves no fields of its own (`src/pay-answers.js`). The first amount is in `unit` (day, month, or total when the offer has a duration).

| Answer | Fields |
|---|---|
| Todo | `payrollMin: false`, `payType` net or gross (the seg «neto, lo que llega» / «bruto, como en la nómina» shows for «Todo» and «Una parte»; without a contract or as autónomo it does not), `payAmount` and `payUnit` = amount and unit, `cashMode: 'amount'`, `cashAmount: ''` |
| El mínimo (el salario mínimo), with an amount | `payrollMin: true`, `cashMode: 'total'`, `totalAmount` and `totalUnit` = amount and unit: the payslip is the minimum, the rest of the total en negro |
| El mínimo, with no amount | `payrollMin: true`, `cashMode: 'amount'`, `cashAmount: ''`, `totalAmount: ''`: all of the pay is the minimum, nothing en negro. The shape the model always had for «the payroll is the minimum» with no cash (`missing` has never asked for a pay there), so no saved format changes |
| Una parte | `payrollMin: false`, `payAmount` = the part, `payType` net or gross, `payUnit` = unit, `cashMode: 'total'`, `totalAmount` and `totalUnit` = amount and unit |
| Sin contrato | `contract: 'none'`, `cashAmount` and `cashUnit` = amount and unit |

«The agreed pay is the minimum» is what lets the cobros be checked against the law alone: in `settle`, the agreed payroll of a month under `payrollMin` (with no net total per day) is that month's share of the agreed minimum, `min.agreed`: the monthly minimum with its two extra pays prorated (2025: 1.184 € × 14 / 12 = 1.381,33 € a month, by hours for part time), for every contract type. Owner decision of 17 November 2026: it is what «cobrar el salario mínimo» means, so a temporary of 120 days or less agrees that too, not the casual per-jornada figure, which is lower. The floor the job is judged against does not change (`min.salary`: per jornada for a casual contract, monthly otherwise), so a short temporary's agreed pay can sit under its floor (many jornadas, few rest days), and the shortfall is then shown. It is the meaning of the existing encoding, so no saved format changes (jobs saved with it two days earlier change figures). «Una parte» plus the payslips shows whether they reach it (net payslips are grossed up by `netFactor` and meet the due). The severance at the end of a temporary contract stays the law's: it is a shortfall of an agreed pay that does not promise it.

En negro is never asked: it is the model's `split.cash`. «Una parte» without the part is missing (`payrollPart`), as the model would take all of it as en negro. The vacation answers are `terms.vacation`; «Se libran, cobrando» is always offered (hidden only for self-employed work): a short or casual temporary can take its vacation days too (a payslip says «Vacaciones 28/30»). `vacationPayable` (`isCasual`: temporary, `natDays` up to `LAW.casualMaxDays`, or a month or less, `isShortContract`) is only where the vacation may also be paid instead of taken, and decides whether paying it is legal in `rights.js` and the hint's wording. Taken, the vacation is paid days off and none is owed (`min.vacation` is 0 and `debt.vacation` 0), casual or not. It does not change the default `defaultVacation` sets (only a month or less).

Shapes the answers cannot say are converted once, in `ingestSaved` (every load path), by `toAnswerable`: a yearly amount becomes monthly, «total» without a duration becomes a month, and a payslip with en negro money, or a part whose unit differs from the total's, becomes «Una parte» with total = net payslip + en negro in the payslip's unit. A gross payslip with no en negro money is «Todo» told gross (`partType` of `answersOf`), not converted. A conversion is kept only if the earnings, gross, net, cash and debt figures stay within a cent. The exception is a net payslip with en negro money and vacation inside the pay: with a total the model puts the vacation on top of the payslip, so both amounts are kept and only that treatment of the vacation moves.

## Rules added in November 2026

- Social Security rates depend on the contract: `ssOf(contract)` in `law.js` (Orden PJC/297/2026), read through `r.pay.rates`. An offer without a contract uses the fijo discontinuo rates, the most common contract in shepherding, to turn cash into gross and to price what the employer saves (owner, 1 November 2026).
- Every ledger's «=» row is the sum of its rows as shown: rows are rounded first, then added, per column (`test/e2e/ledger.js` checks exact equality). A total can therefore differ by a cent or a euro from the same figure computed elsewhere. «Ganado» is not one of those: the headline, Comparar (ranking and head to head) and the share card show the breakdown's sum in their unit (`earnedShown` in `units.js`, with the columns' rounding `shownInColumns`), so they always match; only units the breakdown has no column for (per hour) round the exact figure. The rule lives in `src/ledger.js` (cents, `sum`, `shownDiff`, `fSumE`, days to tenths below 10); views only format what it returns.
- Places: `data/places-<version>.json`, built by `npm run places` from GeoNames (ADR-0015), is fetched when an offer's form opens and kept in Cache Storage under its version; data saving or a 2G line defers it to the location field. `src/places.js` folds names and searches them; a municipality code is named through the list once it has arrived.
- Account sync keeps unsynced changes in `localStorage` (`cj.accountUnsynced`: changed and removed offer ids, settings), so offline edits survive a reload and are written first on the next connect; fingerprints are taken before each write. Signing out writes pending changes first, then clears the browser's copy, as does deleting the account (ADR-0017, ADR-0018).
- Shared rows: `ingestShared` fills the defaults; same-named sharers get a detail (place, then place or dates, then dates, then both, then «n.º N») from `sharedLabels` in `text.js`.
- The schema lives in `supabase/migrations/` (ADR-0016), applied in order by `test/unit/rls.test.js` and by `.github/workflows/db.yml`.

## Public holidays (ADR-0033)

- The dates are data from `kb/tables/holidays.csv`, not law constants: `npm run law-data` writes only the rows with status `verified` into `src/law-territorial.js` (generated, never edited), by community and year (national and regional days) and by municipality INE code and year (the two local days), each with its source id, article and link.
- `holidaysOf({ municipality, province, year })` in `law.js` returns `{ days, local, known }`: the days of the province's community plus the municipality's local days where it has them (`local`), in date order, each with `scope` and `cite`. `known: false` means no verified data for that province or year; nothing is guessed (no patron-saint fallback). The province is the places list's letter code (`terms.province`), the municipality its INE code (`terms.municipality`).
- On the calendar (`marksOf` in `model/record.js`): each day carries `holiday`, the name of the place's holiday (`holidaysOf` with the offer's `terms.municipality` and `terms.province`, the year of the month), or `''` where there is no verified data («si no toca, no toca»). The calendar shows a dot on the day, the name as its accessible label and `title`, and the names of the month under the grid. If the offer takes the holidays off (`terms.holidays === 'yes'`) the holiday dates are the default `libre` and the offer's days off are spread over the other days; otherwise they stay `jornada`, only marked. It is a default of the calendar like the spread of days off: a month the shepherd touched or closed is written out as shown (`freeze`), so a closed month never changes when the data or the offer does, and the offer's counts (`defaultMarks`) use the same rule, so an untouched month shows no difference.
- The model's averages are not replaced by the real count: `schedule` and `settle` keep `LAW.holidaysPerYear × months / 12` for `holidaysOff` (days worked, paid days, due days). Untouched months follow those averages, so the empty-record invariant and the ledgers do not depend on the place or the data; a real count would make the offer's figures (computed before any start date or place is known, and shared with other users' offers) differ with the data available. For a touched or closed month the days are facts, and an offer that takes the holidays off now has them libre there, so the holiday is no longer counted as worked and paid as a holiday too.
- «¿Se cumplió la ley?» has «Festivos trabajados» (`law.holidays` in `lawFromRecord`) only where every closed month's year has verified data for the place, and over closed months alone: the holidays marked `work` in them, with their dates. Information (`?`), not a breach: working a holiday is not forbidden, the holiday is paid and not recoverable (ET art. 37.2) and what compensates it is not in the record; the figure of the compensation is not on the page. Where the province has an agreement article on it (`holidayWorkOf`: Huesca, art. 35) it is named (the cite comes from the verified row `overtime_holiday_premium_pct`), without a figure: the premium is not worded or modelled. With no local days for the municipality the row says they do not appear. No data or no closed month: no row.
- Today: Aragón 2026 (`HU`, `Z`, `TE`) and the 14 pilot municipalities of Huesca. A new community or year is a new set of rows in the table plus, for a new province, its entries in `COMMUNITY_OF_PROVINCE` and `PROVINCE_OF_TERRITORY` in `scripts/build-law-data.js`; callers do not change.

## Saved format and the version gate

- `src/schema.js` owns the saved format. `SCHEMA_VERSION` (9) is what is written to the account's `offers.schema_version` and to backups (`schema_version` beside `offers` and `cfg`). `upgrade[n]` turns version n into n + 1 (`offer` and `cfg`, each optional). Version 1: offers with every field at the top level, settings still holding former law figures. Version 2: `{ id, cmp, terms, mine }` and settings without them. Version 3: `offerOnly` and `place` in `terms` (ADR-0028; migration `20261104120000_offer_only_and_village_in_terms.sql`, `PAGE_VERSION` 2). Version 4: `contract: 'selfEmployed'` and `terms.vatIncluded` (ADR-0029; migration `20261105090000_self_employed.sql`, `PAGE_VERSION` 3). Version 5: `mine.record`, what happened (ADR-0032; migration `20261112120000_record_in_mine.sql`, `PAGE_VERSION` 4): `upgrade[4]` and the migration give every offer an empty `{ months: {}, payments: [] }`, and ingestion normalises it (`normalizeRecord`: unknown day kinds, non-finite amounts, unknown ways and malformed months are dropped, a payment without an id gets one, `DEF_MINE.record` is frozen and every offer takes its own copy). A shared offer has no `mine`, so it gets an empty record. `PAGE_VERSION` and `min_page_version` went up with it because a version-3 page holding an offer in memory (an unsynced edit kept at connect) would upsert `mine` without the record and erase what a newer page recorded; the account's stamp does not protect against it: an old page skips rows stamped above its schema, but its own unstamped in-memory copy is written whatever the row says. Version 6: the claim ladder's fields (ADR-0034; migration `20261114120000_claim_ladder_in_mine.sql`, `PAGE_VERSION` 5), see «The claim ladder». Version 7: `record.complete = { date }`, the record closed («The law, from the record»; migration `20261115120000_record_complete.sql`, `PAGE_VERSION` 6): optional, `upgrade[6]` is the identity and the migration only stamps and raises `min_page_version`, because a version-5 page holding an offer in memory would write `mine` back without it and reopen the record unseen; `normalizeRecord` keeps a valid date and drops the rest. Version 8: `record.paid` (`asAgreed` | `none`) replaces `record.nothingPaid`, a month may hold `count`, a cobro may be `approx` (ADR-0035; migration `20261116090000_record_paid_counts.sql`, `PAGE_VERSION` 7): the migration rewrites `nothingPaid: true` into `paid: 'none'` in every `mine` and deletes `nothingPaid`; `upgrade[7]` does the same for browser copies and backups and must delete it itself, because `normalizeRecord` keeps the fields it does not know. `min_page_version` 7 because a version-6 page holding an offer in memory would write `nothingPaid` back. Version 9: `record.expecting = { by? }`, the counterpart of `complete` (migration `20261117090000_record_expecting.sql`, `PAGE_VERSION` 8): nothing is rewritten, the migration stamps rows 9 and raises `min_page_version` to 8 because a version-7 page holding an offer in memory would write `mine` back without it; `upgrade[8]` is the identity; `normalizeRecord` keeps an object and its valid `by` date and drops the rest. Fixture bundles per version are in `test/fixtures/schema/`, one test per upgrader in `test/unit/schema.test.js`.
- Every load path goes through it: `ingestSaved` (localStorage, backup file, account's own rows) and `ingestShared` (other shepherds' rows). An offer's version is its own `schema_version` (account rows), else its bundle's (backups), else its shape (browser copies were never stamped). Settings have no stamp in the account: they are treated as version 1, and their upgraders are idempotent.
- Writes are always the current version and keep fields the page does not know (`normalizeOffer` spreads them). A row of a newer version is read without its unknown fields, keeps its `schema_version` in memory and in the browser copy, and the account never writes it (`writable` in `storage/account.js`); a row of an older version is rewritten in the current one on connect.
- Browser copies have no stamp and read as version 2; `upgrade[2]` leaves an offer already in version 3 as it is, so that is harmless. For version 2, `mine.offerOnly` wins over a stale `terms.offerOnly` left from before ADR-0017.
- A new version needs: a batch migration that rewrites the rows and bumps `schema_version`, an `upgrade[n]` entry and fixture, and a higher `PAGE_VERSION` with `min_page_version` raised in the same migration when older pages could write the old shape.
- Version gate: `app_meta.min_page_version` (migration `20261104000000_min_page_version.sql`; a one-row table that anon and signed-in users can read and no API role can write) is the oldest page the database accepts. `PAGE_VERSION` in `schema.js`, raised by hand, is this page's. `storage/account.js` reads it on connect and before each flush that has something to write. A page below it stops syncing, reads nothing into the browser, sets the status `outdated` («Hay una versión nueva. Recarga la página.» on the account page and its button) and keeps recording its unsynced changes for after the reload (including the edits made before the account connected); signing out is refused while any are recorded, because it would clear them. A reconnect that finds the minimum lowered syncs again. A project without the `app_meta` table sets no minimum, so the page can ship before the migration is pushed.

## Self-employed (autónomo)

- Entered as the pay answer «Nada: se factura como autónomo» (`contract: 'selfEmployed'`); `payAmount` per `payUnit` is what is invoiced (per day: each day worked), with the IVA inside if `vatIncluded` (ADR-0029).
- `model/self-employed.js`: invoiced = agreed, less the IVA (10 %) if inside. Módulos income («rendimiento neto previo») = invoiced × 0,37. RETA tramo: that income less 7 % generic expenses, divided by the months registered (one without a duration); the quota is the tramo's minimum base × 31,50 % for each month. IRPF: on the módulos income less 5 %, the difference it makes to the year's tax on top of other offers' payslips (`irpfWithActivity`; above 6.500 € of activity income the earned-income reduction of those payslips goes, LIRPF art. 20). What is withheld on invoices (2 %) is shown, not counted.
- In `calc`, a self-employed offer has no payslip (like «sin contrato»), no employee's paro (the «cese de actividad» it contributes to is said, not calculated: at least 12 months contributed and a justified cessation), no severance, debts, savings or unpaid time; `parts.invoice` is what is invoiced and the quota and IRPF are taken off what is earned (`deductionsOf` in `units.js`, shared by the breakdown and `earnedShown`). `r.selfEmployed` holds every step.
- Limits: no en negro beside the invoice; other offers' IRPF ignores the autónomo's income; one activity; the quota at the tramo's minimum base; one tramo for a contract spanning two years.
- **No contract is measured as fixed** (ET 15.4: a worker not registered in Social Security is presumed permanent): `calc` sets the measured contract to `none` (not `temporary`), so the minimum is the monthly one (`minWageOf(year).annual` over the months, never the casual daily figure), the rates are `ssOf('none')` (those of an indefinite contract), there is no severance (a job without contract is not a temporary one) and the vacation owed is that of a permanent job. The ficha says it where the minimum is shown: «Sin contrato se mide como un trabajo fijo (Estatuto, art. 15.4)». The MINIMUM measure itself stays a question for a professional.
- **Minimum wage by year**: `MIN_WAGE` holds 2022 to 2026 (2022 and 2023 read in the BOE text of RD 152/2022 and RD 99/2023: art. 1 per month in 14 pagas, art. 4 the casual daily figure). A job of an earlier year is measured against the first year the page has, and says so (`wageYearNote`: «La página no tiene el salario mínimo de <año>; se compara con el de <año>, más alto»).
- **The value of a vacation day** (`vacationDayOf` in `model/pay.js`, owner decision 15 November 2026: «lo suyo es que valga lo que vale un día»): a vacation day owed is worth the job's real daily pay, cash included (the month's payroll pay plus the cash agreed per month, over `LAW.payrollMonthDays`; every unit reduces to the month so the same offer in another unit is worth the same), and never less than a day at the minimum wage. Payroll and cash are on the same basis, gross, as the minimum wage is: the cash is the gross that brings it net on payroll (`r.cash.grossEq`; without a contract, through the net factor) and a net pay is taken as the gross it was converted to, so a cash of 500 € net adds more than 500 € to the day. The floor of the payslip's own vacation in a net pay is still a net comparison (`payroll`). That is `min.vacationDay` and so `min.vacation` (the ficha's debt, `settle`'s vacation item and early-end vacation, the texts). The payslip's own vacation (`pay.vacationPay`, paid at the end) stays the payroll's part: the payslip cannot depend on the cash, which is derived from it (`effectiveOffer`), so a vacation paid at the end on a payroll below the real pay leaves a shortfall that the cash covers (`settle` moves that much cash from the salary into the vacation, as `calc` does). A vacation agreed «included» in the pay, and the minimum payroll that replaces the pay (`effectiveOffer`), keep a day at the minimum wage as the minimum: the carve-out is the agreed value. The legal claim may only reach the payroll's part: the cash that raises the value of the day is money outside the payslip, so a claim against the ganadero on the cash-inclusive figure is a figure of the page, not of the law.
- **An old job** (`standing.lapsedAll`: something is owed and every month of it is past its deadline): Cuentas says under the yellow block «Plazo para reclamar: pasó el <fecha>» (the last deadline), «Pedir lo que falta» is the informal request only (the talk summary: no legal deadline line, no «Vía legal» button; a dismissal keeps its own line), and the card's status line says «· plazo pasado», or «· hasta el <fecha>» while the nearest deadline is still open.
- **Step 3's sentence under the pay** (`payTold`, `cashLine` in `views/form.js`): a wage per day split into payroll and en negro needs the days worked, which step 4 gives, so until the hours are answered it says «Se calcula con las horas y los días (paso 4).» instead of a figure; with the vacation inside the total it adds «unos N € de vacaciones dentro» so the parts add up to the amount. Without a duration an offer is counted as one month (`schedule`: `noDuration`), as step 2 says.
- **Whole euros in the record views**: the net figures of the Expediente's «Plazos», of «Plazos por mes» and of the month panel of the calendar are whole euros, as the tiles; the months of the deadlines are spread to add up to Falta (`standing.deadlines[].owedWhole`, `spreadTo` of `ledger.js`). The gross tables of the Expediente and the texts of the claim stay in cents: gross is the amount claimed.
- **Small texts of the record**: the papeleta says that after 15 days without a hearing only the caducity resumes (LRJS 65.1: the dismissal's), the prescription of the amounts staying interrupted; the calendar says «Festivo: día 2, Todos los Santos»; the month panel has no «jornadas de más o de menos · 0 €» row where the pay is monthly (the days are in the counts line); the pill is «Sin plus de pernocta»; the debt's stamp and the letter's total say «Falta», the glossary's word.
