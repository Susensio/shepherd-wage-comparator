# Public holidays, Aragón, 2026

## For the page

- Aragón has 12 national and regional holidays in 2026, all paid and not recoverable: 1 Jan, 6 Jan, 2 Apr (Jueves Santo), 3 Apr (Viernes Santo), 23 Apr (San Jorge, the only regional one), 1 May, 15 Aug, 12 Oct, 2 Nov, 7 Dec, 8 Dec, 25 Dec. Rows: `kb/tables/holidays.csv`, territory `ccaa:02`, scope `national` or `regional`.
- 2 Nov and 7 Dec are the Sunday holidays of 1 Nov and 6 Dec moved to the Monday by Decreto 70/2025 («en sustitución del día»). Show the Monday, not the Sunday.
- Local holidays: two per municipality, set by the labour authority, keyed by the 5-digit INE code (`mun:NNNNN`, as in `data/places-*.json`). 14 pilot municipalities are in the table (Huesca province); see the list below. Any other municipality: no data yet, so the ladder of ADR-0033 applies (nothing is invented).
- Do not read a municipality's holidays from this report: read `kb/tables/holidays.csv` rows with status `verified`.

## Facts

- Count: the BOE annex (BOE-A-2025-21667), Decreto 70/2025 art. primero and Aragón's open-data CSV list the same 12 dates. `test/unit/kb.test.js` compares them on every run. The earlier research said 12: confirmed.
- BOE codes: 1 Jan, 3 Apr, 1 May, 15 Aug, 12 Oct, 8 Dec, 25 Dec are national and not replaceable (*); 6 Jan, 2 Apr, 2 Nov and 7 Dec are national with no replacement exercised (**); 23 Apr is the community's (***). The table marks all but 23 Apr `national`.

- BOA 56/2026 is not a correction of BOA 225: it is «complementaria» and adds municipalities that BOA 225 lacks (Bielsa, Biescas and Fiscal among the pilot). No municipality of BOA 56 appears in BOA 225 (checked by name over the 647 entries of both), so no date was changed by it.

## What changes in the model

- The calendar can mark the 12 days for any offer in Aragón, and the two local days where the offer's village (`terms`, INE code) is in the table and verified.
- `LAW.holidaysPerYear` stays the yearly figure for the «¿Se cumplió la ley?» count; the dates are data, not law constants, and come from the generated `data/holidays-2026-02.json` (not built yet).
- A municipality missing from the table shows no local days and says so; it does not fall back to patron-saint guesses (ADR-0033).
- Names: the sources name 13 of the 28 local days; the rest are stored as «Fiesta local».

## Uncertainty

- The owner verified every row on 2026-11-15 («los festivos, si los has encontrado, los doy por válidos»).
- The village name in the offer can differ from the official one: the bulletin and the CSV say «Torla», the places file «Torla-Ordesa» (INE 22230; the CSV leaves its INE code blank). San Juan de Plan and Plan are two municipalities (22207, 22182).
- Aragón's open-data CSV has 687 municipality names and the two bulletins 647: 44 names are only in the CSV (e.g. Arascués, Buera, Nocito, Rasal) and 4 are written differently. The CSV was generated on 2026-05-05, after BOA 56; it may include later changes. Not investigated: none are pilot municipalities.
- 2027: Decreto 163/2026 (BOA 180, 16/09/2026) and BOE-A-2026-21254 are in the manifest and not in the table; the 2027 local list is not published on 2026-11-14.
- Local days can be moved or corrected after publication (as BOA 56 shows); re-check in March.
