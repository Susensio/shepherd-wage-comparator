# Collective agreement terms, province of Huesca, 2026

## For the page

- No figure of the 2022-2025 agreement is in `kb/tables/agreement_terms.csv`: its official text was not found (see Uncertainty). The table has its header only. The page has no checked source for the overnight bonus, the overtime premium, the weekly hours, the vacation or the board and lodging percentages of Huesca for 2022 onwards.
- The agreement is named «Convenio colectivo del sector agrícola de la provincia de Huesca» (convenio code `22000015012004`), not «del campo». Search the BOPH for that name.
- `src/law.js` cites the 2015-2017 text (art. 29, 25 % for sleeping out). That text expired on 2017-12-31 and a 2018-2021 agreement followed it (BOPH 202 of 24/10/2023 names it). Do not present the 25 % as in force on the strength of the 2015-2017 text; the 2022-2025 text has to be found and quoted first.
- Until then, the model can name the bonus only as «según el convenio de 2015-2017, caducado», or leave it out (ADR-0033: no bonus is counted as owed without a known agreement that gives it).

## Facts

- Agreement chain for the sector in Huesca, as far as primary texts show: 2015-2017 (BOPH 58, 29/03/2016, in the repository as expired); 2018-2021 (text not found; the BOPH 202 of 24/10/2023 publishes its definitive 2021 salary tables and says they are the base for 2022 and following); 2022-2025 (known only from the owner's table; text not found).
- 2015-2017, art. 29 (expired): shepherds and cowherds who must sleep away from home for watching or grazing are compensated with a cash amount not below 25 % of the agreement's salary. The wording quoted from the owner's table in `docs/BACKLOG.md` is the same as this article's, which suggests the clause was kept in the later agreements; that is an inference, not a source.
- BOPH 202 of 24/10/2023: the 2021 table lists «Guarda, Peón, Casero, Pastor, Vaquero» at a daily figure and an annual one; the acta fixes the 2021 minimum wage at 13,510 € a year for the categories it affects. These are salary tables, not terms of the concept list, and are not copied to the table.

## What changes in the model

- The territory lookup for agreement terms (`prov:22`) returns nothing today: the overnight bonus must not be added to the minimum owed for any offer on the strength of the repository's sources.
- When the 2022-2025 text is found: rows for overnight bonus (art. on pernocta), overtime, weekly and annual hours, vacation, board and lodging, each with article and quote, `valid_from` 2022-01-01 and `valid_to` 2025-12-31; then ask whether a renewal or ultraactividad applies for 2026 (the term ended on 2025-12-31 per the owner's table).
- Salary tables are a different concept (not in the list): the 2021 table is the base of the later ones, which were not found either.

## Uncertainty

- Not found: the 2022-2025 text. Searched in the daily bulletin pages of the BOPH from 2022-01-03 to 2026-11-13 by title (details in the manifest note of `BOPH-convenio-agricola-2022-2025-candidate`); the BOPH search form needs a captcha and was not used; web search engines were blocked. The agreement may be published under a title without «agrícola» or «campo», in a bulletin page beyond what the scan covered (page 1 only, for broad words, in 2022-2024), or not published in the BOPH.
- The years 2022-2025 and the agreement's name come from the owner's private table; nothing in `kb/` copies its figures.
- The expiry on 2025-12-31 means that in November 2026 the agreement is either renewed, prorogued or in ultraactividad; nothing found says which.
