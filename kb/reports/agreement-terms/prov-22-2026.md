# Collective agreement terms, province of Huesca, 2026

## For the page

- The agreement in force is the «Convenio colectivo del sector agrícola de la provincia de Huesca» 2022–2025 (code `22000015012004`, BOPH nº 131, 9 July 2024). Its art. 4 ends it on 2025-12-31; its art. 5 keeps it applying provisionally, in its own terms, until a new one is signed. No new one was found by 2026-11-15, so it governs in 2026.
- `kb/tables/agreement_terms.csv` holds six rows for `prov:22`, all `verified` by the owner on 2026-11-16: overnight 25 % (art. 28), overtime premium 75 % on working days and 100 % on holidays (art. 35), 1,760 annual hours and 40 weekly (art. 25), 30 natural days of vacation (art. 30). The generator emits the verified rows into `AGREEMENT_DATA` of `src/law-territorial.js`, keyed by province code (`HU`), read through `agreementOf` in `src/law.js`.
- On the page: the overnight bonus (`overnightOf('HU')`, art. 28, link to the BOA copy of the text), now read from its row. It replaces the cite of art. 29 of the expired 2015–2017 text.
- `LAW.annualHours` stays 1,826 (Granada, reference) for every province without a verified row; Huesca uses 1,760 through `annualHoursOf`.
- A shepherd in Huesca working a holiday: art. 35 gives the hours worked that day the 100 % premium on the ordinary hour (overtime hours). The page states the rule only for an offer in Huesca and invents no amount: the salary tables are not modelled.

## Facts

- Chain: 2015–2017 (BOPH 58/2016, expired); 2018–2021 (text not found; its 2021 tables are BOPH 202/2023); 2022–2025 (BOPH 131/2024, this text). The BOA link `https://www.boa.aragon.es/cgi-bin/EBOA/BRSCGI?CMD=VEROBJ&MLKOB=1342050573232` serves the same PDF as the BOPH announcement (same sha256).
- Art. 28: if watching or grazing makes them sleep away from home, shepherds and cowherds are compensated with a cash amount not below 25 % of the agreement's salary.
- Art. 35: overtime premium 75 % on working days, 100 % on holidays, on the ordinary hour; the hourly salary is the annual pay of the category over the annual hours.
- Art. 25: 1,760 hours a year, 40 a week on average over the year; irregular distribution with notice of 5 days; hours beyond the ninth (flexibility A) or the tenth in May–August (flexibility B) are overtime.
- Art. 30: 30 natural days of vacation, which include 22 working days Monday to Friday; official holidays inside the period are not counted as vacation.
- Salary tables, line «Guarda, Peón, Casero, Pastor, Vaquero» (art. 11 item 10 defines the pastor): 2022 14,000.00 € a year; 2023 15,120.00; 2024 (provisional) 15,876.00; 2025 (provisional, BOPH 87/2025, acta of 2025-02-28) 16,576.00, which is the 2025 minimum wage (RD 87/2025): the 3 % rise gave less, so the line was raised to it. The tables are not table rows: salary tables are not a concept of the table.
- BOPH 96/2025 adds art. 56 («Medidas LGTBI»); it touches no figure.

## What changes in the model

- Huesca's annual hours are 1,760 instead of 1,826: the hourly minimum (`min.hourly`) is the year's minimum wage over them (2026: 9.7125 €/h instead of 9.3614), and everything the model prices by the hour follows (extra days and overtime at the minimum, their unpaid amounts, the savings). Other provinces are unchanged. `annualHoursOf(province, year)` in `src/law.js` is the lookup.
- The overnight bonus is counted as before, 25 % of the minimum wage, now read from the row. Rule (owner, 15 November 2026): when an agreement's salary is below the minimum wage the agreement is out of date and the base is the minimum wage; that is what the model does.
- Weekly hours (40) and vacation (30 natural days) equal what the page already uses; the rows are not read for them.
- Not modelled: the overtime premium (75 % on working days, 100 % on holidays). The model values overtime at the ordinary minimum hour and has no place where an agreement premium applies; wiring it would mean a premium on `extra.overtimeValue` for Huesca and wording for it in the claim and the cards, and the premium's base (the agreement's hourly salary, not the minimum wage) is not modelled. The holiday row only supplies the article cited in «¿Se cumplió la ley?» (`holidayWorkOf`), without a figure.
- Not modelled: the agreement's salary tables. The base of the overnight bonus is the minimum wage, by the rule above.
- The Leyes page text (`index.html`) still says the hourly minimum uses 1,826 hours, the Granada reference; it needs a line on Huesca's 1,760.

## Uncertainty

- 2026 salary tables are not published (none found by 2026-11-15). The provisional 2025 pastor salary equals the 2025 minimum wage, so in 2026 the minimum wage governs (RD of 2026) unless a table above it is published.
- Whether a new agreement was signed after 2025-12-31 and not yet published: not known; art. 5 keeps this one applying meanwhile.
- The 2018–2021 agreement is not in `kb/`. The extract of the 2022–2025 text keeps only the articles and annex lines the page reads; the rest was not copied.
- The extract comes from the PDF with its slips as published (for instance «Artículo29», «horas»). The 2025 table has misspellings of its own («Peon», «Aprediz»).
- Row status `checked` meant the script found the quote in the extract; the owner read the articles and verified the six rows on 2026-11-16.
